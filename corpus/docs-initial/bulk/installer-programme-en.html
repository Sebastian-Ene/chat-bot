Aurora Home — Installer Partner Programme

Terms for trade accounts · January 2026

Who it is for

The programme is open to heating engineers and electrical contractors who commission Aurora Home systems for clients. It is not open to end customers, and a trade account cannot be used to buy hardware for your own home at trade terms.

Benefits

  • Extended return window of 45 days on any condition, refunded as account credit.
  • Collection from the Antwerp and Frankfurt depots instead of waiting for delivery.
  • Priority support queue during the heating season, with a same-working-day first response.
  • Access to the handover flow, which transfers property ownership to the client without sharing a login.

Obligations

Partners commission systems in line with the installation guide and hand over ownership to the client at completion. Retaining access to a client's property after handover is a breach of the programme terms: the client cannot remove the account themselves, and support will not remove it without the partner's confirmation.

On-site repair

Partners in Germany and Austria may request on-site repair for commercial installations of twenty devices or more, where a maintenance contract is in place. The visit takes place within five working days of a warranty claim being accepted.

Applying

Apply through the partner portal with your trade registration. Applications are reviewed within five working days. Tier is reviewed annually against the preceding twelve months of volume, and a tier is never reduced mid-year.
