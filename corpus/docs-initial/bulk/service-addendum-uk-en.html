Aurora Home — United Kingdom Service Addendum

Supplement to the warranty, returns and shipping policy · valid from 1 February 2026

Scope

This addendum supplements the general warranty, returns and shipping policy for deliveries to Great Britain and Northern Ireland. Where the two disagree, this addendum governs. Everything not addressed here follows the general policy unchanged.

Shipping and duties

Orders for the United Kingdom ship from the Frankfurt warehouse on delivered-duty-paid terms. Import VAT is calculated at checkout and included in the price shown; couriers do not collect further charges on delivery. Free shipping is not offered for the United Kingdom.

Returns

Consumers in the United Kingdom have 14 days from delivery to return an order, which differs from the general policy. Returns must be booked through the returns portal in the account area: parcels arriving without a returns reference cannot be matched to an order at the border and are refused.

Refunds cover the goods and the import VAT paid at checkout. Original shipping charges are not refunded.

Warranty

Warranty terms follow the general policy. Claims are handled through a service partner in Reading, which typically adds three to five working days compared with claims inside the European Union.

On-site repair is not offered in the United Kingdom.
