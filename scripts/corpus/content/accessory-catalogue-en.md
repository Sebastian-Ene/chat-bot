# Choosing between a repeater and a second hub

A repeater extends the mesh; it does not add device capacity. A property that is
running out of capacity needs a second hub, not a repeater. A property whose
devices drop out at the far end of the building needs a repeater, and adding a
hub will not help — the two symptoms are frequently confused.

# Radiator valves

The AH-RV1 replaces a standard thermostatic head on M30 × 1.5 fittings. Adapters
for older fittings are supplied in the box. A valve counts as a device against the
hub capacity, so a property fitting valves throughout should plan capacity before
ordering.

Valves are battery-powered and report as leaf nodes. They do not relay traffic,
so fitting valves does not improve mesh coverage.
