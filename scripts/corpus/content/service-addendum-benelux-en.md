# Scope

This addendum covers the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg. All three are inside
the EU single market, so no customs charges apply and the general warranty,
returns and shipping policy applies except where stated below.

# Delivery

Orders ship from the Frankfurt warehouse. Standard delivery to the Netherlands and
Belgium takes three to four working days; Luxembourg takes one working day longer
because of the onward leg from the Belgian hub.

| Destination | Standard | Express | Free over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Netherlands | 3–4 working days | 2 working days | €59 |
| Belgium | 3–4 working days | 2 working days | €59 |
| Luxembourg | 4–5 working days | 2 working days | €69 |

# Installer collection

Installer partners in the Benelux may collect stock directly from the Antwerp
depot instead of waiting for delivery. Collections must be booked a working day
in advance through the partner portal, and the collecting driver must present the
order reference.

# Language of documentation

Product documentation ships in English and German. Dutch and French translations
of the installation guide are available on request but are not maintained at the
same revision as the English original; where the two differ, the English text is
authoritative.

# Returns

Return windows and restocking fees follow the general policy. Returns from the
Benelux are consolidated weekly at the Antwerp depot, so a refund typically
completes three to four working days later than one sent from within Germany.
